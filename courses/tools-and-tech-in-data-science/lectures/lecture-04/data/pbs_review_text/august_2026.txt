    Inflation in Brief
    1. CPI inflation General, increased by 11.1% on year-on-year basis in August 2026 as compared to an
       increase of 9.2% in the previous month and 3.1% in August 2025. On month-on-month basis, it
       remained unchanged at 1.2% in August 2026 as compared to the previous month and a decrease of
       0.6% in August 2025.
    2.    CPI inflation Urban, increased by 10.4% on year-on-year basis in August 2026 as compared to an
          increase of 8.7% in the previous month and an increase of 3.5% in August 2025. On month-on-month
          basis, it increased by 0.9% in August 2026 as compared to an increase of 1.2% in the previous month
          and a decrease of 0.7% in August 2025.
    3. CPI inflation Rural, increased by 12.2% on year-on-year basis in August 2026 as compared to an
       increase of 9.9% in the previous month and an increase of 2.5% observed in August 2025. On month-
       on-month basis, it increased by 1.6% in August 2026 as compared to an increase of 1.2 in the previous
       month and a decrease of 0.5% in August 2025.
    4.    SPI inflation on YoY basis increased by 9.5% in August 2026 as compared to an increase of 12.0%
          in the previous month and an increase of 2.6% in August 2025. On MoM basis, it increased by 0.9%
          in August 2026 as compared to 2.4% in the previous month and an increase of 3.2% in August 2025.
    5.    WPI inflation on YoY basis increased by 11.8% in August 2026 as compared to an increase of 9.4%
          in the previous month and a decrease of 1.0% in August 2025. On month-on-month basis, it increased
          by 2.0% in August 2026 as compared to no change in the previous month and a decrease of 0.2% in
          August 2025.
    6.    Figures 1 and 2 show graphically the yearly data of Table 1.a for Urban and Rural CPI while Table 1.1
          shows “Period Average” and “YoY” percentage changes in indices.
