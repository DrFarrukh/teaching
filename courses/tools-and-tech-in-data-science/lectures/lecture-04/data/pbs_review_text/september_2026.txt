    Inflation in Brief
    1. CPI inflation General, increased by 10.3% on year-on-year basis in September 2026 as compared
       to an increase of 11.1% in the previous month and 5.8% in September 2025. On month-on-month
       basis, it increased by 1.3% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of 1.2% in the previous
       month and an increase of 2.1% in September 2025.
    2.     CPI inflation Urban, increased by 10.1% on year-on-year basis in September 2026 as compared to
           an increase of 10.4% in the previous month and an increase of 5.7% in September 2025. On month-
           on-month basis, it increased by 1.3% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of 0.9% in the
           previous month and an increase of 1.6% in September 2025.
    3. CPI inflation Rural, increased by 10.5% on year-on-year basis in September 2026 as compared to
       an increase of 12.2% in the previous month and an increase of 5.9% observed in September 2025.
       On month-on-month basis, it increased by 1.2% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of
       1.6% in the previous month and an increase of 2.8% in September 2025.
    4.     SPI inflation on YoY basis increased by 8.4% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of
           9.5% in the previous month and an increase of 4.5% in September 2025. On MoM basis, it increased
           by 1.0% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of 0.9% in the previous month and an
           increase of 2.0% in September 2025.
    5.     WPI inflation on YoY basis increased by 13.3% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of
           11.8% in the previous month and an increase of 0.6% in September 2025. On month-on-month
           basis, it increased by 1.9% in September 2026 as compared to an increase of 2.0% in the previous
           month and an increase of 0.5% in September 2025.
    6.     Figures 1 and 2 show graphically the yearly data of Table 1.a for Urban and Rural CPI while Table
           1.1 shows “Period Average” and “YoY” percentage changes in indices.
