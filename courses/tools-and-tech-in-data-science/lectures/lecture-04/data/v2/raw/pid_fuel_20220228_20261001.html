  •   PM's HEALTH INSURANCE SCHEME FOR JOURNALISTS & MEDIA WORKERS  

PR No. 255
Islamabad: February 28, 2022

The global petroleum products’ prices are tracking the Ukraine- Russia war and resultantly surged to $100/bbl. The unprecedented increase is very risky for the domestic fuel prices and inflation. The situation leaves very few options for the Government. Prior to today’s review, the Government has left more than Rupees 70 billion per month to keep the prices lower and providing relief to the masses. In the fortnightly review, due on 28th February, OGRA has recommended Rs10/litre increase in the petroleum products’ prices. The Prime Minister has not only rejected the increase but also announced to decrease the prices of petroleum products by Rupees 10/litre in his address to the nation in order to provide maximum relief to the consumers, despite the limited fiscal space. The new prices of petroleum products with effect from 1st March, 2022 will be as follows:

ABOUT US

Press Information Department is the principal department of Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, headed by Principal Information officer (PIO). PID is working since 1947 with the mission to establish an authentic source for timely dissemination of information to people through all forms of media.

PID carries out its operation round the clock through a proper mechanism of media projection, monitoring and feedback. It aims to publicize the government policies and also to apprise the Government about the impact of its policies.
