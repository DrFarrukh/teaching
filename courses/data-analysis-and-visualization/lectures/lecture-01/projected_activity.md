> New and electric vehicle brands are breaking the dominance of Pakistan's traditional Big Three.

|---|---:|---:|---:|

## Coverage note

The source reports production and sales for named models and categories in a PAMA workbook. It may not include every imported vehicle, nonmember firm, new entrant, or government registration.
